About

I am a freshwater ecologist and environmental scientist with a focus on greenhouse gas emissions from aquatic ecosystems — lakes, ponds, streams, and wetlands. My research investigates how carbon dioxide (CO₂) and methane (CH₄) are produced, transported, and emitted from freshwater environments, and how human activities and climate change alter these fluxes.

I combine field-based measurements (including custom-built sensor systems), large-scale data synthesis, and statistical modelling to understand freshwater carbon cycling. I am also committed to reproducible, open science — sharing data, code, and tools openly through GitHub.

Research Themes

🌊

Greenhouse Gas Fluxes

Measuring and modelling CH₄ and CO₂ emissions from lakes, ponds, streams, and wetlands, with a focus on ebullition vs. diffusion pathways and temporal dynamics.

🔬

DIY Sensor Technology

Designing low-cost, open-source sensor systems for automatic measurement of methane and CO₂ fluxes in aquatic environments, enabling high-resolution temporal monitoring.

🏞️

Lake Restoration & Ecology

Investigating nutrient dynamics, phosphorus cycling, water quality, and biodiversity in newly established and restored lakes, with long-term monitoring data from Danish lakes.

🌍

Global Synthesis

Contributing to global datasets and meta-analyses of freshwater carbon dynamics — including a worldwide dataset of depth-resolved CO₂ and CH₄ concentrations across 522 water bodies.
